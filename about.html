About Harivo

We connect car washers with customers — no queue, no waiting.

Harivo is not a car wash. It's a platform that connects professional car washers with customers who need them. Browse, compare, and book a wash in one tap — verified pros, instant booking, zero hassle.

Our story

Built to solve the car wash booking problem.

Car washers struggled to find customers. Customers hated waiting in queues. Harivo was born to connect both sides — a platform where car washers list their services and customers book in one tap. No middleman, no waiting, just a simple connection.

Whether you're a professional car washer looking for more bookings or a car owner who values convenience, Harivo makes the connection effortless.
